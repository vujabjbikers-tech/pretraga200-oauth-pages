PRETRAGA200-BACKUP

PRETRAGA200-BACKUP is a personal, owner-operated backup integration for storing and restoring backups of the PRETRAGA 200 project in the owner's Google Drive account. The connection is configured with rclone, a command-line file synchronization tool.

The application requests Google Drive access to create, list, read, and manage backup files as directed by the account owner. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to outside users.
