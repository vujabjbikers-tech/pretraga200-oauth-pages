Privacy Policy

PRETRAGA200-BACKUP is a personal Google Drive backup integration operated for its account owner's use.

Google Drive access and use

The integration requests Google Drive access to upload, download, list, and manage project backup files at the account owner's direction. The requested Google Drive permission may allow access to the entire Drive, not just a dedicated backup folder.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in the Google Drive account chosen by the owner. This public information website does not accept file uploads, collect Google Drive files, or operate a server that receives Google Drive data. The integration is not designed to sell or share Google user data with third parties. The owner's local rclone configuration is used for authorization.

Retention and control

The account owner manages backup files and their retention in Google Drive. The owner can revoke application access through Google Account settings and can delete stored backups using Google Drive.

Contact

For inquiries about this personal integration, contact the owner through the support address shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
